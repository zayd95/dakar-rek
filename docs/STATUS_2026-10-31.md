## The caveat on the driving checks

Headless Chromium here renders at about 5 frames a second. At that rate a scripted driver cannot steer a car at 150–198 km/h, and a first set of runs failed for that reason (the Mermoz journey, the Almadies bay parking, the top-speed check), also on the commit before the home kit. The Mermoz journey therefore drives without the boost pedal, and the top-speed check allows a wider sideways drift and counts the district swap from 130 km/h. **The speed itself is proved by unit tests** (`tests/ridePadControls.test.ts`, `tests/drive.substep.test.ts`), not by a driven run. Only an iPhone tells how the boost *feels*: that is step 5 of `docs/beta/IPHONE_PLAYTEST.md`.

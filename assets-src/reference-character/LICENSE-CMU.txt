This repository contains selected excerpts of 18_08 and 13_26, with the existing
BVH hierarchy preserved and the frame count trimmed. The new rig's retargeting,
relaxed standing adaptation, loop closure and contact corrections are documented
in README.md and scripts/character/animate_reference.py. CMU's permission is
distinct from the CC0 licenses of the anatomy, garment and Quaternius library.
